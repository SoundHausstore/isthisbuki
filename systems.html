Systems

I did not start with agents. I started by getting work to run without me.

It began on my laptop, moved to a remote server, and now reports into Discord.

Each agent has one job and only the tools that job needs. Nothing is published or sent without my approval.

How it grew
  1. My laptopFive content agents: research, copy, design, quality control and feedback
  2. A dashboardTo start the agents and approve what they make
  3. Publishing and leadsBuffer to seven social channels, and agents that find DJs, agencies and labels
  4. A remote serverRendering, agents and schedules moved off the laptop
  5. DiscordThe system reports to me there, and I answer from there
  6. On its ownDaily scheduled jobs, an hourly health check, and agents for my own career
How Discord works
  1. An agent runsOn a schedule, on the server
  2. It posts to DiscordInto its own channel
  3. I answerA message, or approve or reject on each item
  4. It actsRemy drafts only what I approved
