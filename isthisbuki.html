isthisbuki

Every isthisbuki artwork is a real photograph from my life.

No photoshoots, no AI, nothing made up. Just what was in front of me.

Most of them are blurred, because they were taken in the moment, not posed for.

The blur is the point. It shows the moment was real.

isthisbuki is my own project, and it is about human imperfection.

It is not trying to be perfect. It is trying to be me.

Ten tracks are finished and unreleased. Four of them are here.
